Common questions

My workouts aren't on my other device

Workouts sync through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud is on for DropSet in Settings → your name → iCloud. Settings in DropSet shows the last sync.

Heart rate or calories are missing

Turn on Apple Health in DropSet's Settings and allow heart rate and active energy. A watch's heart rate can take a few minutes to arrive after a workout. Health data stays on each device, so each one reads its own from Health.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the menu on their post or profile and choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Your profile, posts, likes, and follows are removed from the server. Your workouts stay in your iCloud.

How do I restore or cancel DropSet Plus?

Restore from the DropSet Plus screen in Settings. To cancel, open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.
