DropSet

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

DropSet is a workout tracker. Logging lifts works without an account, and what you log stays in your own iCloud. The social features are optional and use an account on DropSet's server. This policy explains what goes where.

What stays on your devices and in your iCloud

Your workouts, sets, templates, programs, personal records, body measurements you log in the app, and settings are stored on your device and synced between your devices through your private iCloud database. DropSet's developer can't see this data. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, DropSet asks for permission to:

  • Read heart rate, active energy, body weight, body fat percentage, and the calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat you log in other apps.
  • Write your workouts and the body measurements you log in DropSet.

Data read from Apple Health is kept only on the device that read it. DropSet never stores it in iCloud, never sends it to DropSet's server, never puts it in posts, and never uses it for advertising or shares it with anyone. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app.

Your DropSet account (optional)

To follow people and post workouts, you sign in with Apple or Google. DropSet's server, hosted by Supabase, then stores:

  • An account ID and the email address Apple or Google provides. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real address; Apple then gives a private relay address.
  • Your profile: handle, display name, bio, avatar choice, website, social links, and whether your account is private.
  • Who you follow, who follows you, who you've blocked, and the posts you like.
  • Workouts you choose to post: the title, lifts, sets, reps, duration, your note, and weights unless you hide them. Nothing is posted unless you tap Post.
  • If you turn them on: your total workout count and weight lifted, and the program you follow.
  • Reports you send about other people's posts or profiles.
  • A push notification token for each device, so you can be told about likes and posts.
  • When you accepted the Terms of Use.

Your handle, display name, bio, avatar, website, and links are visible to other DropSet users. Posts are visible to the audience you pick for each one. If your account is private, only people you approve see your posts.

What DropSet doesn't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • No selling or renting your data.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the server and database for social features.
  • Apple provides iCloud, Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and in-app purchases. DropSet never sees your payment details.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google, if you use it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes your DropSet account from the server right away, including your profile, posts, likes, follows, blocks, and device tokens. If you signed in with Apple, DropSet also revokes its access to your Apple ID. Reports you sent stay, without your name, so moderation records remain complete. Copies in server backups are gone within 30 days.

Your workouts stay in your iCloud until you delete them, or the app itself.

Children

DropSet's social features are for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
